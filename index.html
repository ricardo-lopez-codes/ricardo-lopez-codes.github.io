ADAPTIVE AI HET (HALL EFFECT THRUSTER) TESTBED

An intelligent autonomous testbed integrating digital twin modeling and reinforcement learning to optimize Hall-Effect Thruster (HET) performance for space propulsion systems. The framework replaces risky manual engine tuning with predictive plasma control and continuous closed-loop telemetry analysis. Designed for safe high-voltage operations, it accelerates satellite propulsion R&D while drastically reducing testing costs.

FIELDEYE - AI DRIVEN PLANT HEALTH MONITORING SYSTEM

An autonomous edge-AI agricultural rover designed for real-time crop health assessment, pest identification, and targeted pesticide intervention. Built on a dual-microcontroller architecture (ESP32-CAM & ESP8266) with an onboard model trained on 11,000 images, it eliminates heavy reliance on cloud servers. Features complete grid navigation, multi-lingual farmer advisory support, and dual-mode (autonomous/semi-autonomous) operation.
